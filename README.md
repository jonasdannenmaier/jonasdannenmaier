* eine App, die deine Woche lernt und von selbst plant

## English

Founder of [Ampena](https://ampena.com/en). Bring on tomorrow.

Ampena is an iPhone app that plans charging for your electric car: at home in the cheapest hours, on the road along your route. You say when you want to leave, Ampena plans the rest.

### What I'm building

* Home charging plans based on wholesale power prices and green power in the grid
* Charging stops on the road that fit your route and battery
* evcc and Home Assistant on your home Wi-Fi: read-only, the values stay on your iPhone
* An app that learns your week and plans on its own

### Try it

The beta runs on TestFlight, currently in Germany only: [ampena.com/en/test](https://ampena.com/en/test)
